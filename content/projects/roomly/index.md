Takes shared bills and chores out of the group chat: housemates record who paid, settle a debt only when the other side confirms, and see chores, events and open bills on one calendar. Splits must balance to the cent, enforced in Postgres.

## The problem

Shared expenses live in a group chat and a half-remembered mental ledger. Chores
rotate until somebody stops believing it is fair. Every existing app solves one
of these and asks the household to install three.

## What I built

One app with expenses, chores, calendar and shopping in the same account.
Splits must balance to the cent or the write is rejected by Postgres, a
settlement is only recorded when the person who received the money confirms it,
and recurring expenses are generated by a scheduled idempotent job instead of
being retyped monthly.

## Implementation

- Invariants in the database: Row-level security plus constraints that make unequal splits balance and stop a debt being settled twice, so no client path can write an inconsistent ledger.
- Settlement needs both sides: Either flatmate can record a payment, but it only closes when the recipient confirms it, which is the part every splitting app leaves to trust.
- Chores that stay fair: Assignment, recurrence and rotation with a weekly leaderboard, so the rota is visible rather than remembered.
- Greek and English throughout: Full i18n including date and currency formatting and due-date wording, tested per locale.
- Designed, not themed: A cream and ink visual system built on semantic design tokens, replacing brand-specific utility classes so components carry meaning instead of a colour name.
